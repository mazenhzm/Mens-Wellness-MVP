# 21. AI MUST NOT CREATE MEDICAL TREATMENT

This is a hard architectural rule.

The LLM must NEVER independently invent a treatment plan.

The AI can:

* explain approved educational content
* explain the user's existing plan
* motivate adherence
* answer wellness questions
* summarize progress
* help users reflect
* suggest discussing concerns with a professional
* propose non-medical plan adjustments for backend validation

The AI cannot:

* diagnose
* prescribe
* change medication dosage
* guarantee outcomes
* claim to cure disease
* replace a healthcare professional
* independently create medical treatment
* directly mutate the user's plan/database
